# Research and existing evidence

Research questions concern sound composition of non-equivalent modality evidence
and acquisition of adequate evidence without unnecessary reauthentication.
Cryptography, IFC, attestation, barcode authentication and database consistency
are established foundations. Any novel contribution must be demonstrated against
strong controls rather than assumed from a new interface or project name.

Existing evidence: [OpenAVT authority experiment, revision 38beb67](https://github.com/ketanbj/openavt/blob/38beb670688e77910b29ada475a8deddb0169575/docs/experiments/authority-2026-10-31.md).
It contains 12 local trials, 60 fresh audio calls and 264 cases. Its deliberate
post-check revocation schedule exposes a synthetic check-to-effect gap in both
TLS and QUIC. It is not a physical deployment or visual/tactile result.

The workspace voice pin is the published main merge of the QUIC implementation;
the experiment above is a separate public evidence revision. There are no camera
or tactile benchmark measurements in the new modality scaffolds.
