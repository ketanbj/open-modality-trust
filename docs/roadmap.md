# Research roadmap

- Months 1-3: formalize guarantee/assumption types, composition, freshness,
  purpose binding and action contracts; build conformance cases and strong IFC
  and authorization controls; map the existing voice implementation.
- Months 4-6: shared verifier prototype and OpenAST signed-code baseline, followed
  by a live camera/display challenge and an audio/visual workflow.
- Months 7-9: selective evidence acquisition, handoff invalidation, AgentCore/Cedar
  and a participating backend; OpenATT simulations and a bounded tactile fixture.
- Months 10-12: held-out attacks, real channel trials, cross-model evaluation,
  reproducible evidence and a manuscript.

These are proposed research milestones. New repository scaffolds do not establish
implementation, novelty, funding or measured security. Tactile scope is feasibility
and limitations, not a general touch-security stack.
