# Research roadmap

## Implemented local foundation

- Shared exact-action snapshots, bounded signing profile and per-modality key maps.
- Separate one-use owner grants, durable revocation/replay state, and local receipts.
- OpenAST signed visual claim protocol and real QR raster encode/decode pipeline.
- OpenATT bounded CSV/force processing, controller signatures and pattern checks.
- Reproducible generated-channel, finite policy and local concurrency benchmarks.
- Source-hash checks, public synthetic fixtures and research draft papers.

These are engineering reference implementations. They do not establish novel
cryptography, physical association, human consent or a general composition result.

## Proposed research milestones

- Months 1-3: formalize composition/context/assumption rules and dependency
  invalidation; establish strong typed-IFC/authorization controls and voice mapping.
- Months 4-6: extend the shared verifier, live camera/display capture and
  voice-to-sight handoffs; measure real physical-channel reliability.
- Months 7-9: selective acquisition, multi-agent handoffs, AgentCore/Cedar and
  participating backends; a bounded tactile capture fixture.
- Months 10-12: held-out/adaptive attacks, model-driven comparisons, formal
  analysis and reproducible manuscript/artifact releases.

Planned targets are not measurements, funding or submission claims. Tactile scope
is bounded feasibility and limitations, not a general touch-security stack.
